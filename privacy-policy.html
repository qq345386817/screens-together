Privacy Policy

ScreensTogether is designed for local nearby-device playback. The app does not require an account for the core host and display room workflow.

Data used by the app

ScreensTogether uses local network access to discover nearby devices and synchronize playback state between a host device and display devices. Imported video content and room state are handled locally on your devices for the playback session.

Data collection

ScreensTogether does not sell personal data and does not use the local display room workflow to create an external user profile.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@luopeike.com.
