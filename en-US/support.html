Support

For support, email support@luopeike.com. Include the device models, iOS/iPadOS/macOS/tvOS versions, room layout, content type, and whether the problem affects the host or a joined display.

Helpful details

  • The layout selected on the host, such as 1 x 2, 1 x 3, or 2 x 2.
  • Whether all devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • A screenshot or short video of the host room and display assignment screen.
